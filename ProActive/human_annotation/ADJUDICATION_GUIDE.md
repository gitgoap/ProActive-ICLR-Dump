# Short adjudication guide

Adjudication starts only after all three independent files are complete.

1. Merge the files.
2. If at least two annotators chose the same `label6`, use that majority label.
3. If all three differ, reread the visible evidence together and enter one
   allowed label in `adjudicated_label6`.
4. Stay blinded: do not open the private key or reveal dataset/model names.
5. Do not change the original annotator cells. They are needed to calculate
   agreement.

Low agreement is a result, not a reason to rewrite annotations.
